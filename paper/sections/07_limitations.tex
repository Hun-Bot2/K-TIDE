\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Single domain, single model.}
The study covers the material of one course and one 1.5B base model, so the findings may not transfer to other domains, languages, or model sizes.

\paragraph{LLM-judge evaluation.}
Our main metric is a rubric-based LLM judge without reference answers.
LLM judges are noisy, and in a pilot, re-judging identical answers changed sub-scores for most items.
We mitigate this with repeated judging, reference-based metrics and a human agreement check, but judge bias cannot be ruled out.

\paragraph{Teacher-written knowledge.}
Canonical knowledge units rely partly on the teacher model's own knowledge.
Cross-model verification and manual review reduce errors but cannot rule them out.
